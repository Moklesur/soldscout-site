SoldScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 (version 0.1.0)

SoldScout is a Chrome extension that helps eBay sellers research sold prices and profit. This page explains what data SoldScout uses, where it is kept, and what we never do.

Short version

  • SoldScout works only inside your own browser. It has no server and no account.
  • SoldScout does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no cookies. We never sell data.

What SoldScout reads

When you open a page on www.ebay.com, SoldScout reads the public product information on that page to show its card: for example the title, price, shipping, sold count, watchers, seller name and feedback, condition, and the prices and dates on a sold items search. It only reads pages you open yourself. It never opens or loads eBay pages in the background. This information is used on the spot to show the card and is not sent anywhere.

SoldScout does not run on any other website, and it does not read your eBay account, messages, orders, passwords or payment details.

What SoldScout stores

SoldScout keeps data with Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), which stays on your computer:

DataWhenWhy
Saved products: item number, title, price, currency, shipping, sold count, watchers, seller name, date saved Only when you press "Save product" To show your saved list and let you download it as a CSV file
Settings: default fee %, fee per order, currency Only when you save the Settings page To fill in the profit calculator

The numbers you type into the profit calculator are not stored. CSV files are made on your computer and saved where you choose.

Sharing

SoldScout does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone. The links on the card (for example "Sold listings") open normal eBay pages in a new tab only when you click them.

Deleting your data

  • Delete one saved product with its Delete button in the side panel.
  • Reset settings with Reset to defaults on the Settings page.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all of its stored data.

Children

SoldScout is a tool for sellers and is not made for children under 13.

Changes

Future versions may add optional online features (for example an account or AI advice). Before any data leaves your browser, we will update this policy, show the change in the extension, and ask for your permission where the law or Chrome requires it.
