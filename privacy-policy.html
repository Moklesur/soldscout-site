SoldScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 (version 0.1.0)

SoldScout is a Chrome extension that helps eBay sellers research sold prices and profit. This page explains what data SoldScout uses, where it is kept, and what we never do.

Short version

  • SoldScout works only inside your own browser. It has no server and no account.
  • SoldScout does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no cookies. We never sell data.

What SoldScout reads

When you open a page on www.ebay.com, SoldScout reads the public product information on that page to show its card: for example the title, price, shipping, sold count, watchers, seller name and feedback, condition, and the prices and dates on a sold items search. It reads pages you open yourself, with one exception below, and never crawls eBay. This information is used on the spot to show the card and is not sent anywhere.

Recent sales on a listing: when you open a listing, SoldScout loads that same listing's public purchase history page on www.ebay.com (the page behind the "X sold" link) to count how many sold in the last 7, 14 and 30 days. Chrome sends this request to eBay like a normal visit to that page from your browser. SoldScout only does this for the listing you have open, reuses a count for 6 hours, and loads at most 6 such pages a minute. The page is read on your computer; it is not sent to us or anyone else. SoldScout never reads buyer names from it.

The side panel shows which kind of eBay page your current tab is (for example a listing or a sold search). It only looks at the address of eBay tabs. Chrome does not show SoldScout the address of tabs on other websites.

"Check sold on eBay" in the right-click menu: when you select text on any website, right-click and choose this menu item, Chrome gives SoldScout only the text you selected. SoldScout opens an eBay sold items search for that text in a new tab. The text goes only to eBay, in the search address, just as if you typed it into eBay yourself. SoldScout does not read the rest of that page and does not store the text. You can turn the menu off on the Settings page.

SoldScout does not run on any other website, and it does not read your eBay account, messages, orders, passwords or payment details.

Sharing

SoldScout does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone. The links on the card (for example "Sold listings") open normal eBay pages in a new tab only when you click them.

Deleting your data

  • Delete one saved product with its Delete button in the side panel.
  • Untrack a seller with its ★ button in the side panel's Sellers tab, or press Delete there to remove everything SoldScout remembers about that seller.
  • Reset settings with Reset to defaults on the Settings page.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all of its stored data.

Changes

Future versions may add optional online features (for example an account or AI advice). Before any data leaves your browser, we will update this policy, show the change in the extension, and ask for your permission where the law or Chrome requires it.
